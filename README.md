## What is LibreSuite?

LibreSuite provides a unified environment where teams can:

- Communicate in real time
- Manage tasks, projects, and workspaces
- Plan schedules and events
- Create and maintain internal or external documentation
- Share a single source of truth across the organization and teams

All within one platform, backed by open standards and open source, and fully self-hostable.

## Why LibreSuite?

LibreSuite exists because collaboration tools became expensive, fragmented, and increasingly restrictive. Over time, essential features turned into paid upgrades, teams were pushed into tiered pricing, and simple workflows started to depend on monthly invoices instead of actual needs.

LibreSuite takes a different path. It is open source, self-hostable, and modular by design — so you run only what you need, pay only for your infrastructure, and stay in control of your data.

## Core Principles

- Open Source — fully transparent, forkable, and community-driven
- Self-Hostable — you own your data and infrastructure
- Modular Architecture — services and packages evolve independently
- Developer-First — built with modern TypeScript tooling
- Deterministic Builds — powered by Turborepo caching
- Scalable by Design — ready to grow from local development to production
